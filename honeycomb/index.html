A Sweet Secret in Sanewadi

A boutique micro-patisserie built for pure indulgence.

Located inside Radha Housing Society on Seasons Road, Honeycomb was born from a singular obsession: elevating desserts into memorable art pieces paired with serious specialty coffee.

We bake our golden, flaky butter croissants fresh every morning, hand-spin our gelato flavors, and extract espresso shots with micro-calibrated precision. Whether satisfying a late-night sweet tooth or meeting for a tranquil afternoon brew, Honeycomb is your sweetest neighborhood retreat.

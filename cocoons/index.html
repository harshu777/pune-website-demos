The Story Behind Cocoons Fashions

Established in 2006 in Aundh, Cocoons Fashions Private Limited was founded with a singular mission: to make shopping for kids and family celebrations delightful, high-quality, and stress-free.

Located in Showroom No. 1, Trade Field Building (opposite Blackberrys on DP Road), Cocoons has dressed thousands of Pune children for their first birthdays, school annual functions, family weddings, and festive pujas. We ensure every garment is made with breathable lining, safe non-scratchy embroidery, and custom fit adjustments on-site.

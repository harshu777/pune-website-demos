The Old Skool Ethos

Where college nostalgia meets serious cafe comfort.

Tucked into Chaitraban Residency along ITI Road, Café Old Skool is built for the post-workout recharge, late-afternoon study sessions, and heartfelt weekend reunions with old friends.

We believe that comfort food should never cut corners. Our patties are pressed fresh to order, our sauces are simmered from scratch, and our specialty coffee blends deliver that authentic roast kick that keeps Aundh coming back day after day.
